QUESTIONS

What does forging cost?
Nothing. The forge takes no payment and the artist earns nothing from it. You pay only the network's gas fee for your own transactions.
What happens to the two pieces I put in?
They are burned. Permanently. Their token ids are retired, they leave every wallet and every marketplace, and nothing can bring them back. This is the whole point: supply only ever falls.
What do I get?
HORIZON: one new piece, composed from both. The bass comes from your first piece, the high notes from your second, and the two maps blend into one field split at the horizon line. It is a real Audio Map with a real token id, minted to your wallet in the same transaction that burns the parents.
Does the order matter?
Yes. Bass and top are different roles. The same two pieces the other way round make a genuinely different artwork. Preview both orders before you burn.
Why is my rare greyed out?
The 52 moments are pieces of Ethereum's history that were dealt once and can never be minted again. The forge destroys what goes into it, so the contract refuses them outright. This is enforced on chain, not just here.
Can I forge a HORIZON?
No. A forged piece cannot go back into the forge. The contract refuses it.
Why did maxSupply change to 1,500?
Contract plumbing, not the collection. Token ids come from a counter that never rewinds even when pieces burn, so forged pieces need id headroom above 1,000. Nobody can mint into that room except the forge, and the forge only shrinks the collection: 1,500 is the ceiling if every single piece were burned two-for-one. The count you see on marketplaces only falls.
Is the approval safe?
Approving lets the forge contract burn Audio Maps from your wallet — but only inside a forge transaction that you sign, only pieces you chose, and it can do nothing else: the contract has no withdraw, no payment, no owner mint, and its full source is verified on Etherscan. You can revoke the approval any time from your wallet or revoke.cash.
Can the forge be switched off?
The artist holds exactly one power: closing it, forever. There is no reopen. He cannot mint through it, cannot burn anyone's pieces, and cannot touch what you own.
