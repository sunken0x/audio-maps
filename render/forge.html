Order matters. A gives the bass, so swapping them makes a different piece, not the same one backwards.

How much each parent is heard depends on the pair. Both are always present, but where A is a long-note piece its bass arrives as one sustained note a bar rather than a moving line.

Moments cannot be forged. The 52 rares can be rendered and heard like any other piece, but the forge consumes what goes into it and a moment cannot be minted again. This page refuses them.

The forge is live. Composing here burns nothing — but once your wallet is connected and it holds both pieces, the panel above the notes offers the real thing. The contract is verified on Etherscan: no payment, no owner powers except closing it forever, moments refused on chain.
